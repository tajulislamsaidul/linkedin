🚀 Turning research into a practical, explainable AI demonstration one step at a time.
I’m excited to share our final research defence project: Explainable AI based deep learning use for cancer detection and stage classification
As part of our research, we focus on the practical implementation and visualization of AI assisted lung CT image analysis. We developed our own custom models, LungCANet and LungCANLite, and compared their performance with other models. Our interactive application serves as a demonstration tool to explore and present our research findings, representing the implementation aspect of our broader research project.
💻 Our implementation supports the demonstration of our research outputs through several technical components:
1.	 Lung CT image preprocessing and enhancement
2.	AI based classification into Benign, Malignant, and Normal categories
3.	GradCAM visualization to explore image regions influencing model predictions
4.	Comparative evaluation of custom and other AI models
5.	Ensemble prediction workflows and result visualization
6.	Analysis history and research report generation 
Beyond the implementation, this project has been valuable learning experience in Machine Learning, Explainable AI (XAI), Computer Vision, Medical Imaging, software development, and research oriented problem solving.
One important lesson we learned is that research is not only about developing a technical approach; it is also about communicating its implementation, visualizing its outputs, and understanding the importance of rigorous validation.
LungCANet is a research and educational prototype, not a clinical diagnostic system. Its purpose is to support the demonstration and exploration of our research implementation.
